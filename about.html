About

Five people from Franklin, Tennessee, and the records that raised them.

Who We Are

Neon Belly is a five piece out of Franklin, Tennessee. We do not come from the same place, the same jobs, or even the same corner of music. We landed in the same room because of one thing we all had in common: the rock we grew up on.

That means the 90s and 2000s. Grunge, alt rock, punk, and the heavier stuff that came with it. The songs that were on every radio station, every burned CD, and every long drive. We play them loud, we play them tight, and we play them like they still matter, because to us they do.

All five of us have been in the band since 2026. It is a young band with a long list of songs behind it, and we are just getting started.

What You Get

A set built out of the songs people actually want to hear, not deep cuts nobody knows. Expect Nirvana, Sublime, Foo Fighters, Alice in Chains, Green Day, Weezer, Stone Temple Pilots, Megadeth, 3 Doors Down, and plenty more. We have played everything from two hour sets to four hour nights, and we are comfortable filling a room for as long as you need us to.
