About Neon Belly

Neon Belly is a Franklin/Nashville-area rock band built around the music that defined the 1990s and 2000s: hard rock, grunge, metal, and alternative rock. The set is loud, high-energy, and audience-focused, leaning on the songs a room already knows by heart while carrying enough weight (heavier riffs, real dynamics, a full-volume back half) to stand apart from a standard bar-rock night. Five players, one shared record collection, and a set built to hold a room for a full night rather than coast through the hits everyone expects and stop there.
