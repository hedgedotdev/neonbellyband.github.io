About

Driven by high-octane energy and a deep-rooted sense of groove, Nate is the thunderous heartbeat behind Neon Belly. Sitting behind the kit with thirty-five years of drumming history, he blends hard-hitting, expressive live energy with an unwavering commitment to a tight, immovable pocket. Raised on a steady diet of symphonic percussion in his youth and sharpened through years of gigging across the Nashville music scene, Nate pulls heavy influence from powerhouses like Dave Grohl, Chad Sexton, and Zac Farro, bringing the raw drive needed to lay down signature anthems from Foo Fighters, Paramore, Toadies, and beyond.

Off the throne, Nate serves as Neon Belly's strategic compass, drawing on his extensive experience in the Middle Tennessee music circuit to refine the band's live product, setlists, and venue strategy. For Nate, it's about more than just playing the notes, it's about delivering a polished, high-voltage rock show that leaves every room buzzing long after the final crash dies down.
