About

Brett grew up surrounded by the music that would quickly take over his life. Everything from Metallica to Michael Jackson had him hooked from the moment he first heard it as a kid. He picked up the guitar at 14 and started learning riffs from bands most kids his age had never even heard of, an itch to discover new music he has never quite been able to satisfy.

Brett brings a level of beef and attitude to the Neon Belly guitar sound that can't be replicated. Leaning back into the rhythm section for the most part, he has his moments of flash with quick, flashy soloing. Catch him on stage headbanging along to all the rock classics.
