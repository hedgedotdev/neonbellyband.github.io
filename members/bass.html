About

Chris grew up on a skateboard and a steady diet of punk rock, and the music just kept getting heavier as he got older. Eventually that pull landed him in a hardcore band, and the stage went quiet for a long road while career and family took the steering wheel.

Music was always a driving force, and the pull never went away. Now he is back in the front seat playing bass with Neon Belly, holding down the low end on the songs he grew up yelling along to. Everything from Weezer to Alice in Chains, and he is happiest when the set swings toward the heavy end.
